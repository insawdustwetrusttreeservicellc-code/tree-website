Common questions

How much does tree removal cost in Pinellas County?

Every job is different. Tree size, access, how close the tree is to your house or fence, and cleanup all change the price. We give free estimates so you get a real number for your property.

Do you remove hazardous or storm-damaged trees?

Yes. If a tree is leaning, split, dead, or damaged by a storm, call us and describe the situation. We can talk through the best way to handle it.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Pinellas County, Florida, including Clearwater, Dunedin, Palm Harbor, Safety Harbor, Oldsmar, Tarpon Springs, Largo, Pinellas Park, Seminole, and St. Petersburg. Call to confirm service for your address.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree?

Rules vary by city and by the type of tree. Ask when you call and we can talk through what may apply to your address.

Can you work on tall trees close to a house?

Yes. We work with a bucket lift and by climbing, which lets us trim or take down trees close to roofs, fences, and yards.
